   → "只有傻瓜才拿了枪...一会儿打鸟，一会儿打野猪"
   → He focuses on ONE market, ONE setup type

5. **The hunter waits in silence. Most of the time, doing nothing.**
   → His trading frequency: 4-6 trades/month
   → 90% waiting, 10% executing

### What This Means for Replication:
Any automated system based on his method MUST have a "wait mode" as the DEFAULT state. The system should spend most of its time saying "no" to setups, only activating when the prey is fully in range.

## 4. THE "TOO闯出" JUDGMENT — A New Concept

**First pass missed this entirely.**

闯出 (chuǎng chū) = "rushing out" / "too aggressive"

This is his term for entering BEFORE the setup is fully confirmed. He uses it in multiple contexts:

1. NEAR entry: "我觉得现在还是有一点太闯出" → Price at 2.099, trigger at 2.1
   → He waits for 0.001 more. THAT's how precise his patience is.

2. General principle: Entering before the candle closes = 闯出
   → "减肥" (on a diet) = candle still forming, not ready

3. Entering before price reaches the Fibonacci zone = 闯出

**This is a CRITICAL behavioral rule:** His edge comes as much from what he DOESN'T do (enter early) as from what he DOES do.
