## GAP 2: "Fat Candle = No Pullback" Rule (FH15)

**What He Said:**
> "你看他如果是这样子胖胖的一根蜡烛，没有真的好，那这个波段可能是很认真的，要继续往下跌，他都不回头了" (11:45)

**What We Missed:**
This is an EXPLICIT candle pattern rule that was not fully extracted. A "fat" (thick-bodied) candle at the end of a swing signals STRONG MOMENTUM CONTINUATION. The market "不回头" (doesn't look back) — no pullback, no retest, just continuation.

This is the OPPOSITE of the "美女" (hammer) pattern:
- 美女 = thin body + long wick = REVERSAL
- 胖胖的 = thick body + small wicks = CONTINUATION

**The Continuation vs Reversal Candle Rule:**
```
At a key S/R level:
  IF candle has SMALL body + LONG wick (美女) → REVERSAL likely
  IF candle has THICK body + SMALL wicks (胖妞) → CONTINUATION likely
  IF candle is "很认真的" (very serious/determined) → No pullback expected
```

**Update Required:** Add to candlestick pattern judgment section.

## GAP 5: Fibonacci for Consolidation/Ranging Markets (FH22)

**What He Said:**
> "从上一个波段的低点拉到最新的盘整波段。你看整整副都是受力范围" (19:51)

**What We Missed:**
There are TWO different Fibonacci drawing methods:
1. **Trending market:** Draw from swing high to swing low (or vice versa) — this is what Week 1 taught
2. **Consolidation market:** Draw from previous wave LOW to current range HIGH — this is DIFFERENT

For Silver (consolidation): He drew from the PREVIOUS swing low to the TOP of the current range. This captures the ENTIRE consolidation zone as the "hunting range."

**The Silver Fibonacci Drawing:**
```
Consolidation Fibonacci Method:
  Anchor 1: Previous completed wave LOW (left side)
  Anchor 2: Top of current consolidation range (right side)
  Result: Entire range becomes "狩猎范围" (hunting range)
  Key levels within range: 0.786 retracement (78.6%) as trigger
```

**Update Required:** Add consolidation Fibonacci method to Fibonacci section.

```

VALIDATION: "所以它现在上涨是逻辑的合理的"
  — The upward move is LOGICAL and REASONABLE because the breakout
    was tested and held. This is not speculation — it's pattern confirmation.
```

**The "逻辑的合理的" (logically reasonable) Test:**
He evaluates setups by asking: "Is this move logically justified by the pattern?" If yes → high conviction. If no → wait or pass. This is his internal validation mechanism.
