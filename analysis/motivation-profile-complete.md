## THE CORE REVELATION

**He doesn't trade for money. He trades to prove he is no longer the person he once was.**

His deepest drive is REDEMPTION — social redemption. He comes from a world he now mentions with clear disgust: multi-level marketing (传销), "分本金" (splitting principal), "你推荐我，我推荐他" (you recruit me, I recruit him). In Chinese context, MLM carries deep stigma. It consumes social relationships. It sits in a moral gray zone.

He is ESCAPING from that past and differentiating himself from it through every trade, every lesson, every chart.

> "正规的金融市场当中，用技术去赚钱" — In legitimate financial markets, use SKILL to make money.

Every word carries weight. "正规" (legitimate) vs illegitimate. "技术" (skill) vs zero-skill recruitment. Trading gave him what MLM never could: **the dignity of a craft.**

**Teaching as self-discipline:** "讲课也不简单，说实话。" Teaching forces him to systematize his knowledge. He can't just act on intuition — he must explain WHY. This makes him a better trader.

**Teaching as salvation:** He genuinely wants students to escape the same traps he fell into.
> "我还是比较希望大家能学习到这门技能，因为只要学习到这门技能，大家在国内的生活...你都会很轻松，因为我们不需要再去看...分人家的本金。"

Translation: *I want you to master this skill so you never have to do what I once did.*

**Teaching as identity:** He is "执行总裁" — Executive President. A solo trader has no title. Teaching gives him a public identity — Topman 高总, respected, authoritative.

**Teaching as compensation:** In MLM, he profited from downlines. In trading education, he delivers a skill that makes students INDEPENDENT. His ultimate goal is: they no longer need him. This is what separates his teaching from his past.

**Money is a scorecard, not the goal.** His checklist never starts with "how much can I make." It starts with Trend. S/R. MACD. Fibonacci. Candle.

> "赚钱是结果，你把事情做正确了，你才得到那样的结果" — Money is the result. Do things correctly, then you get that result.

**The 26x BTC trade is not described in dollars.** He describes the PROCESS: 45D entry, zero-axis regression, adding at each touch, MACD divergence, 415-day decline formation. He remembers the trade through its structural architecture, not its profit.

**Money is a tool.** "用合约赚来的国库资金回收豪门币" — Trading profits flow into the community treasury. This transcends personal enrichment.

**He is not driven by greed.** He repeatedly warns against it. In an industry driven by greed (going long), he constantly hedges against it. This detachment may come from: he already has enough, or money is merely the byproduct of his true pursuit (redemption), not the goal itself.
