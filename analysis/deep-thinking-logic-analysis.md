## PART 6: THE POSITION SIZING PHILOSOPHY

### 6.1 The Deeper "Why" Behind 50 Parts

"多给自己一点机会，给自己10次机会"

The 50-part (large) / 20-part (small) / 5-part (tiny) system isn't just about risk management. It's about:

1. **PSYCHOLOGICAL SURVIVAL**: If you have only 1-2 "bullets," every trade is life-or-death. You'll overtrade, undertrade, exit early, hold losers. With 10-50 bullets, you can think clearly.

2. **OPPORTUNITY ENDURANCE**: "它最重要的不是说你500就能赚多一点...它最重要的东西是这个东西：盈亏比例"
   → The goal isn't to maximize per-trade profit. It's to SURVIVE long enough for the 1:10+ trade to appear.

3. **EMOTIONAL DETACHMENT**: When each trade risks only 2-10% of capital, you can follow the system without fear. Fear causes premature exits and missed entries.

### 6.2 The 1:3 Minimum — Why This Number?

"1:3来，是我的基础范围"

1:3 is his MINIMUM because:
- At 30% win rate (which he considers realistic), 1:3 is PROFITABLE
- At 40% win rate (his typical), 1:3 is COMFORTABLY profitable
- It allows for 7 losing trades out of 10 and STILL makes money
- It accounts for the "犯错一定很多" (many mistakes) reality

1:10 is his TARGET because:
- It allows for 90% LOSS RATE and STILL profitability
- One 1:10 trade pays for 9 losers
- This is how he achieves 40%+ monthly ROI with only 4-6 trades/month

### 6.3 The "Almost翻仓" Setup — What Makes It Different

When he said "我一个单我可以几乎翻仓的状况":

He's describing a setup where:
- MACD on a MAJOR timeframe (45D, 4D) shows divergence at zero axis
- Price is at a MULTI-YEAR S/R level
- Fibonacci confluence from a massive wave
- The R:R is 1:10+ naturally (the target is far because the structure is huge)

These setups are RARE (maybe 1-2 per year). His system is designed to:
1. SURVIVE with small 1:3 trades
2. STAY IN THE GAME long enough
3. POUNCE with size when the 1:10+ setup appears
