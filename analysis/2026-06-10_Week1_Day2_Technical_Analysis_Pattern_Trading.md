## 3. Core Technical Content

### 3.1 Triangle / Consolidation Patterns (盘整形态 — 三角形)

**Three types taught:**

| Type | Description | Trading Rule |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| **上升三角形** (Ascending) | Flat top, rising bottom in an uptrend consolidation | DON'T predict direction. Wait for breakout, then follow. |
| **下降三角形** (Descending) | Flat bottom, declining top in a downtrend consolidation | Same rule — follow the breakout direction |
| **对称三角形/楔形** (Symmetrical/Wedge) | Both sides converging equally | Follow breakout — could go either way |

**Critical Teaching Point:**
> "不要去背死书，有些人可能会觉得是上升三角形，它就一定会要上涨，不一定的。不要去管它是什么类型的三角形，它往哪里突破，我们就往哪里去看。" — Don't memorize books rigidly. Some think "ascending triangle means it must go up" — NO. Doesn't matter what type it is, where it breaks, we follow.

**Drawing Method:**
1. Find TWO connecting swing points (highs or lows) — draw first trendline
2. Find the opposite TWO connecting points — draw second trendline
3. WAIT for the THIRD touch point to confirm the line validity
4. Wait for BREAKOUT, then enter in breakout direction

**Candle confirmation on breakout:**
> "蜡烛很粗很大支的，它会跟你说它的意思它很有力量，它要往下走了" — A very thick, big-bodied candle tells you it has POWER and is going to move in that direction. After breakout, candles come in thick consecutive bodies ("pop pop pop").

### 3.3 Timeframe Hierarchy (时间级别)

The trainer uses a **map navigation** metaphor:
- **Big timeframe** = Google Maps overview (Guangzhou to Shenzhen — one straight route)
- **Small timeframe** = Turn-by-turn navigation (turn left here, turn right there, detours)

**Official Hierarchy:**

| Tier | Timeframes | Purpose | Label |
|------|------------|---------|-------|
| **Macro Trend** | 月图 (Monthly), 周图 (Weekly), 日图 (Daily) | Determine overall trend direction | 看趋势 |
| **Trading Zone** | 4H, 1H | Identify support & resistance levels | 看支撑阻力 (交易时间) |
| **Entry Execution** | 30m, 15m | Precise entry timing | 看进场 (进场时间) |

**Trainer's nuanced view:**
- Monthly candle takes 30 days to close → trading off monthly alone means you might wait a year for entry
- Weekly takes 7 days per candle — institutional level, Buffett style (but too slow for crypto)
- **4H and 1H are the MOST IMPORTANT** — this is where S/R is precisely defined
- 30m and 15m are for entry refinement; going smaller introduces too much noise

**Why NOT to trade short timeframes:**
> "小时间是很杂的...短线太多不应该的信息出来了...方向是一样的，要下跌，但是你看短线走了很多冤枉路才走下来...它可以直接从这里拿到这里结束。" — Small timeframes are chaotic. Too much irrelevant information. Direction is the same (down), but short-term charts take many detours. Price could go straight from A to B, but short-term traders get chopped up on every wiggle.

**Key insight on short vs long timeframe:** The trainer showed overlapping lines from Monthly → Weekly → Daily → 4H — same overall direction, but each smaller level adds more "noise" (小的波段). The smaller you go, the more "wrong paths" (冤枉路) you'll walk.

### 3.4 Fibonacci Retracement Tool (斐波那契回调)

**Tool Setup:**
- Located in TradingView: Left sidebar, 3rd panel, first tool
- Trainer's custom levels: 0, 0.236, 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786, 1, -1.618
- He added one extra level beyond default (customized)
- Settings: Extend to right (未来), remove background color, place labels on right, font size 10-11

**Core Usage Method:**
1. Identify a completed wave (波段): LOW → HIGH (for uptrend) or HIGH → LOW (for downtrend)
2. Draw Fibonacci from LEFT (past) to RIGHT (future): bottom → top of wave
3. The retracement levels (0.5, 0.618, 0.786) define the **hunting range** (狩猎范围)
4. Wait for price to enter the hunting range
5. Look for reversal candle confirmation (美女/吞没) at these levels
6. Enter when price reacts at a Fibonacci level

**Critical Rules:**
- Always draw from left to right (past to future)
- Fibonacci defines a PROBABILITY ZONE, not a guaranteed stop point
- When a new wave breaks the old structure, extend Fibonacci to the new wave
- Tool works in BOTH directions (retracement for continuation, extension for targets)

**Trainer's exact words:**
> "它只是个狩猎范围，不是说价格一定在那里停止...他一进入我们的狩猎范围，我们就干嘛？猎杀他嘛！我们现在像猎人一样，他进入我们的狩猎范围，我们就猎杀他" — It's only a hunting range, not a guaranteed stop. Once it enters our hunting range, what do we do? Hunt it! We're like hunters — when it enters range, we hunt it.

**Real example given:**
- BTC $17,000 call: Price hit a Fibonacci retracement zone → trainer went long → rode to $100,000+

### 3.5 Channel Trading (通道)

**Bitcoin predominantly forms CHANNELS, not triangles:**
- Channels = two parallel boundaries with equal width
- BTC liquidity profile creates more channel-like behavior than gold/silver
- "可能它的流动性比较稍微比黄金白银少一点，所以做盘的那种方式" — Perhaps BTC's slightly lower liquidity vs gold/silver creates this channel tendency
- As more institutional money enters crypto, triangles may become more common
