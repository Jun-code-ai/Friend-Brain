# BOOK 1: HIS WORLDVIEW

## How He Sees the Market

### The market is NOT random. It is a HUNTING GROUND.

He doesn't see candlestick charts. He sees a landscape with:
- **Trails** (trends) that prey follows
- **Watering holes** (support/resistance levels) where prey gathers
- **Blind spots** (liquidity zones above/below obvious levels) where prey gets ambushed
- **Hunting ranges** (Fibonacci zones) where the hunter positions himself

### The market has PREDATORS and PREY.

| Predators (Institutions) | Prey (Retail Traders) |
|--------------------------|----------------------|
| Think in weeks/months (45D, 4D) | Think in minutes/hours (15m, 5m) |
| Accumulate at zero-axis | Buy at the top, sell at the bottom |
| Push price to grab liquidity at obvious levels | Place stops at obvious levels (hunted) |
| Move price in ONE big wave | Get chopped up on every wiggle |
| Are patient (months of accumulation) | Are impatient (need action NOW) |

**His edge:** He thinks like a predator while most traders think like prey.

### The market LEAVES FOOTPRINTS.

- **MACD零軸 = The institution's average entry price** on that timeframe
- **EMA52 = The price-level equivalent** of the MACD零軸
- **When price returns to零軸 = The institution is DEFENDING their position**
- **Divergence = The institution is LOSING momentum** — they're distributing, not trending

### The Three Laws of Market Behavior (His Implicit Theory)

**Law 1: Price Moves in Waves, Not Lines**
"趋势往哪里走，我们跟哪里就对了"
→ Accept the wave. Don't fight it. Don't predict its end. Ride it.

**Law 2: Levels Attract Price**
Price WILL revisit key levels. The question is WHEN, not IF.
His job: identify WHICH levels matter, then wait.

**Law 3: Time Determines Behavior**
Different sessions produce different behaviors.
21:00 US open = liquidity grab pattern.
London session = steady moves.
Asia session = low liquidity, false signals.
