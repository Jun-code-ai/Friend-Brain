## 1. Timeframe Hierarchy

| TF | Purpose | Action |
|----|---------|--------|
| 1M | Macro trend context | Confirms overall direction |
| 1W | Structural trend | Major S/R levels |
| 1D | Trend confirmation | MACD 24/52 direction |
| 4H | Structure analysis | FVG detection, S/R flip, breakthrough |
| 1H | S/R precision | Fine-tune support/resistance levels |
| 30m | Entry planning | Candlestick confirmation |
| 15m | Entry execution | Precise CE retrace entry |

## 2. Decision Sequence

1. Check 1M/1W/1D for trend direction (MACD 24/52)
2. Identify key S/R levels on 4H/1H
3. Find active FVGs on 4H
4. Wait for price to retrace to CE level on 30m/15m
5. Confirm with candlestick pattern at CE
6. Set stop at structural swing behind gap
7. Set TP based on playing range and next S/R
8. Trail at 3R+ with momentum evaluation
